About Our System

Our Crop Recommendation System is designed to assist farmers in making informed decisions. By analyzing critical factors such as soil composition, temperature, rainfall, and humidity, the system suggests the most suitable crops.

We leverage machine learning to enhance crop yield and sustainability, providing region-specific tailored recommendations.
